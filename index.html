Sourdough 101

Sourdough Fundamentals

For complete beginners. No experience needed.

We'll walk through the fundamentals together, from caring for your starter to mixing, shaping and baking a beautiful, crusty loaf. It's fully hands-on and built to give you the confidence to bake at home.

What you'll learn and take home

  • How to maintain and feed a sourdough starter
  • Proper mixing, shaping and baking techniques
  • Your own dough to bake later at home
  • A portion of my sourdough starter to keep baking
  • A starter kit with everything you need to keep going

Bring: just yourself. You'll need a Dutch oven at home to bake your loaf.

Sourdough 201

$80per person

Discard Recipes & Everyday Baking

For 101 grads, or anyone who already feeds and keeps a starter.

Go beyond the classic loaf. Together we'll make individual sourdough cinnamon rolls and mix a sourdough pizza dough for you to bake for dinner, then taste a variety of discard appetizers.

What you'll learn and take home

  • How to use discard in sweet and savory recipes
  • Individual sourdough cinnamon rolls made in class
  • Sourdough pizza dough to bake later at home
  • A tasting of sourdough discard appetizers
  • A recipe bundle full of discard-friendly ideas
  • More confidence using your starter between loaf days

Bring: your 101 kit and starter, or your own equivalent setup.

Questions

Which class should I take?

If you're new to sourdough, start with Sourdough 101. Sourdough 201 is for anyone who has taken 101 or already knows how to feed and maintain a starter.

Do I need any baking experience?

Not for 101. It's built for complete beginners, and we go step by step.

What should I bring?

For 101, just yourself. We supply everything except a Dutch oven, which you'll need at home to bake your dough after class. For 201, bring your Sourdough 101 kit and your starter, or the equivalent supplies if you have your own setup.

Where are classes held?

In Clermont, FL. You'll see the address on your confirmation page right after booking. Before class, I'll send a text to confirm you're coming and explain how to get into the neighborhood without any trouble.

Is food included?

Yes. Every class includes light appetizers centered around sourdough, with fresh bread baked ahead of time.

What if I can't make it?

Classes are non-refundable. If something comes up and you can't make it, please reach out as soon as possible at ryleesbake@gmail.com.
